> The river was wider than I remembered.
> I pushed the old boat off the bank and it rocked.
> I sat still and let it settle.
> The water knocked softly against the wood.
> The far bank was green.
> The oar was heavy in my hands, and it was mine.
> I pulled once, and the boat moved.

A short text for make.fun: a wide river, an old boat, and one pull of the oar. Fear first, then stillness, then the water holding the wood — and a single brave stroke at the end. The entry program (`main.py`, Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes the text to `entry.txt`.
